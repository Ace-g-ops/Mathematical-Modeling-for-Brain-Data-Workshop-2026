# Hubert Apana

- **Session:** Linear Regression and the General Linear Model
- **Role:** Computer Engineer

## Biography

Hubert is interested in how neural complexity gives rise to cognition and behaviour, and how embodied cognition shapes intelligent systems that interact with the world. As a computer engineer with an MSc in Engineering AI from Carnegie Mellon University, he developed expertise in AI systems, data pipelines, and full-stack engineering. Currently, he is working with intracranial neural data, studying how the brain encodes and sustains information over time. He has trained at the Simons Computational Neuroscience Imbizo. His technical interests span memory, learning, meta-learning, and neurorobotics. He is passionate about making computational neuroscience accessible to African students.
