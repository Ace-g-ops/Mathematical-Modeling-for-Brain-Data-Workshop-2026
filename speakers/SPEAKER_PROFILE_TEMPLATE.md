## Biography

Kira Düsterwald is a South African medical doctor, computational neuroscientist, and healthtech founder. She has recently submitted her PhD at the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit at University College London, UK, where her research uses behavioural modelling, EEG, and computational approaches to study how humans learn structure, make predictions, and adapt to uncertainty. Kira has clinical experience in paediatrics and emergency medicine, including work in the UK's National Health Service and in rural South African healthcare settings. She is a teaching assistant for the Computational Neuroscience Imbizo and organiser for the Deep Learning Indaba. Alongside her academic work, Kira is the CEO of Amathambo AI, a health technology company developing tools for patient-load prediction and clinical workforce planning in African health systems. Amathambo won the
Mandela Rhodes Foundation's Äänit Prize in 2023.
