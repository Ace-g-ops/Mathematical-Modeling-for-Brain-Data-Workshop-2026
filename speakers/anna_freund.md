# Anna Freund

- **Session:** Calculus and Differential Equations for Neural Dynamics
- **Affiliation:** [Institute of Neural Engineering, TU Graz](https://www.tugraz.at/institute/ine)
- **Role:** Project Assistant

## Biography

Anna Freund is a project assistant in the team of Dr. Kerstin Lenk at the Institute of Neural Engineering, TU Graz. She received her master’s degree in Biomedical Engineering with a major in Computational Neuroscience from the Technical Universityof Graz. Her research centers on computational modeling of brain function across
multiple scales, with a particular focus on astrocytes and their role in neural signaling and neurological disease. Her work ranges from single-cell models of astrocyte morphology and compartmental calcium signaling to large-scale brain network models informed by neuroimaging data.
